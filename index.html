My journey into Machine Learning

Hi there! 👋 Welcome to my journey into Machine Learning!
My purpose is to write a logbook about my studies, which started officially during Summer 2022, collecting and reporting all the the most sound resources I cross in my way.


We live in a world flooded with data.
In 2018, the total amount of data created, captured, copied, and consumed was 33 zettabytes and is predicted to reach 175 ZBs by 2025!
Machine Learning has evolved as an AI branch to extract knowledge from data and make decisions/previsions: not only it’s fundamental in scientific research but plays an important role in our life too (voice and text recognition, medical machinery, browsers, spam filters, invincible chess opponents…)
